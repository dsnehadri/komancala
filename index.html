rules

Pick one of your six pits and drop its stones one at a time, counter-clockwise, into every pit and your own store — never theirs. Land your last stone in your store and you go again. Land it in an empty pit on your side and you capture that stone plus everything in the pit across from it. When one side runs out, the other player banks whatever is left. Most stones wins.
